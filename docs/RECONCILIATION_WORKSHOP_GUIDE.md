# Resolve the remaining uGP–AM parts

Open AM through Nexus using your own account, then open [RET–AM workshop](https://am.1pwrafrica.com/admin/reconciliation.php?queue=1). An AM approver hosts the session; involve RET where the question calls for engineering judgement.

1. Open **Work still to resolve** and choose **Review this requirement**. Read the specific question and who needs to answer it. The queue uses your accessible inventory; its counts are not physical-stock balances.
2. Read the uGP requirement and specification. **View this UGP part, assemblies and drawings** opens its engineering context. Only this one requirement is being reviewed.
3. Search AM and inspect the candidate tiles. Read **Original AM identity** and the recorded description, check its tag and photographs. A published name alone does not establish equivalence. You can page through all results.
4. Select only records that represent the same specified part. **Compare before publishing** repeats the uGP requirement alongside the selected AM descriptions. A warning prevents known conflicting pairs from publication.
5. Choose the correct decision:
   - **Same part**: the physical item and specifications agree. Select the requirement in the confirmation field, record the actual evidence, name the RET participant present, and complete the three checks. Publication preserves the recorded AM description and original identity; the shared definition supplies the canonical engineering specification.
   - **Possible alternative**: for example, an M16×380 bolt when the design specifies M16×200. Record the engineering question, a named owner and due date. This is not an identical-part publication.
   - **Component of assembly**: for example, a spindle is only one part of an insulator assembly. Record the components and quantities needed, owner and due date. A kit cannot be counted from each component separately.
   - **Need a specification/photo**: record the specific missing measurement, model label or evidence. **Start a follow-up for this question** pre-fills a draft; replace it with findings before saving.
   - **No existing item found**: save the search outcome and assign an owner. This does not mean zero stock or that the item was never purchased.
6. Save. Follow-up decisions appear in **Follow-up queue**; saving does not message the owner. Agree the assignment directly. Use **Open next unresolved requirement** to continue. Moving between requirements never saves a draft.

Example: choose the complete 11kV dropout cutout record for the dropout requirement. A 5A fuse link is a component and must not be published as the entire cutout. Similarly, a surge arrester is not a steel cross-arm, even if somebody previously renamed it.

Known conflicting records remain blocked for that specific requirement. Do not rename them to bypass the warning. RET and the catalogue steward must resolve an engineering-source conflict; an approved alternative remains an alternative until represented properly in the design/catalogue. The stay-wire ID/name disagreement requires correction in uGP first.

Photographs identify a part type; they do not establish quantities, receipt, location or ownership. Shared photos can be reused across countries. AM-only supplies need no uGP link. After a valid publication, capture a new Nexus Forecast snapshot; older snapshots remain unchanged.
