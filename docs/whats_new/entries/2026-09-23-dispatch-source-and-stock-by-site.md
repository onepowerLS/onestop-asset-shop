Dispatch now issues from headquarters unless you choose another store, and item pages show total on-hand plus a per-site breakdown.

Fulfilling a request no longer moves the catalog “current location” to the last destination, which had made a small site quantity look like the whole stock. Existing requests without a saved source store also issue from HQ.
