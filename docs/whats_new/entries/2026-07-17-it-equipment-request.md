IS&T equipment requests can now be forwarded directly to Asset Management.

When an IS&T staff member forwards an equipment ticket, AM creates a dedicated IT Equipment Request workflow. AM managers can review, approve, reject, or fulfill the request, and the status syncs back to the IS&T ticket.
