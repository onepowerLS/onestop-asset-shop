AM approvers can verify supported UGP mappings and record receipts for enrolled purchase orders.

Receipts update stock and order coverage together. Partial receipts hold closeout;
full returns retain audit history. Inconsistent stock requires reconciliation.
The help and tutorial pages include English and French instructions.
