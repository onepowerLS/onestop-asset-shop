Catalog search now ranks matches and understands plurals and a few related words, so a search for drones also finds drone and UAV.

The catalog opens with a search box. Each word must match the name, aliases, manufacturer, model, tags, notes, or category. Results say where they matched, and the closest hits are listed first.
