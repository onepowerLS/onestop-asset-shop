When Asset Management blocks an action, the message now shows the user’s effective assignment, the role or capability required, and the correct access owner to contact.

The guidance distinguishes country HR assignment changes, explicit Nexus/IS&T access, and protected Asset Management administrator roles, and reminds users to sign in again after a correction.
