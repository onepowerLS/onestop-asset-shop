AM approvers and RET colleagues can reconcile the 36 MAS part requirements against
live inventory tiles, publish identical parts with one shared description and number,
and assign unresolved questions. Approved reference photos can be reused across countries.
The AM help/tutorial contains the workshop steps and a safe component-versus-clamp example.
