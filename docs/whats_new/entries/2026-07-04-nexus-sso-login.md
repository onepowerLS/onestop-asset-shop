Asset Management now signs you in through the 1PWR Nexus portal.

If you open AM while signed out, you're taken to Nexus to log in with your
usual 1PWR credentials and returned to AM automatically — one login for all
1PWR tools. The AM login form remains available in emergencies at
/login.php?fallback=1.
